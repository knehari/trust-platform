# Conceptual persistence model — v0.1

Entities:
- `ca`: immutable CA identity and active policy reference.
- `certificate_request`: requester, CSR fingerprint, idempotency key, status and policy decision.
- `serial_reservation`: unique (issuer_id, serial_bytes), allocated before signing, never reused.
- `certificate`: issuer, serial, DER fingerprint, lifecycle status and publication state.
- `revocation`: certificate identity, reason (0-6, 9, 10; never 7 or 8), invalidity/revocation timestamps (both limited to 1970-01-01 .. 9999-12-31, revocation date from migration 0016; the invalidity date is never after the proposal or decision; so every CRL can encode them), `change_id` of the approved status change. Definitive revocations (reason other than 6) are irreversible; a hold (6) may be released or upgraded. Status transitions go through `certificate_status_change` (maker-checker, migration 0014). From 0018 the lifecycle audit decision names the change and reason (`REVOKE:applied:change=<uuid>:reason=4`).
- Privileges (0023, ADR-0006): the revocation/CRL tables and functions are owned by the NOLOGIN role `trust_revocation_owner`; service functions are `SECURITY DEFINER` with `search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp`; the capability roles `trust_revocation_officer_api`, `trust_crl_signer`, `trust_crl_operator` and `trust_ca_admin` hold EXECUTE on their functions only (plus SELECT on `ca_revocation_policy` / `crl_publication` for the officer API / signer) and no table DML. Superusers and the owner still bypass this.
- Issuance privileges (0035): the issuance functions (`create_issuance_request`, `transition_issuance_request`, `reserve_issuance_serial`, `create_signing_intent`, `mark_signing_uncertain`, the 8-argument `store_signed_certificate`, `quarantine_stale_signing_intents`) are owned by the NOLOGIN role `trust_issuance_owner`, `SECURITY DEFINER` with the pinned `search_path`; the capability role `trust_issuance_service` holds EXECUTE on them only. The owner has SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on the request, serial and intent tables, SELECT/INSERT on `certificate`, INSERT on `audit_event`, SELECT on the policy-gate tables and UPDATE on `ca.policy_gate_epoch` only. Since 0043 the policy-administration functions (observation, CP/CPS draft, draft approval, gate activation, presign grants, dual-signature receipts and enforcement) are granted to `trust_policy_admin`, and the reconciliation and evidence functions to `trust_reconciliation_operator`. Both are EXECUTE-only, and the functions are owned by `trust_issuance_owner`.
- Precautionary holds (0036, ADR-0007 §6): `ca_revocation_policy.precautionary_hold_hours` (NULL = off; requires suspension with a CPS reference) and `certificate_status_change.confirm_by`. A precautionary hold is the only APPROVED change without a checker, and the CHECK ties that to `confirm_by` (reason 6, no invalidity date, deadline after the decision). Its single MAKER signature is stored in `status_change_signature` through a `PRECAUTION` challenge. `precautionary_holds(ca)` lists holds whose revocation row is still the hold's, i.e. not yet revoked or released under dual control. 0038: `precautionary_hold_limit` (default 3) caps one officer's open holds per CA, checked on insert under a per-officer advisory lock.
- Signed CA-admin rejection (0042): `ca_admin_challenge` purpose REJECT and `ca_admin_signature` role CLOSER. On a signing CA, `reject_ca_admin_change`, `reject_credential_change` and `reject_admin_credential_change` refuse, and `reject_signed_admin_change` closes the change.
- Evidence audit (0041): `signed_evidence_index(ca)` (family, change, status) and the signature and claim read functions are granted to `trust_audit_verifier`.
- Admin keys under dual control (0040): `ca_admin_credential_change` (append-only, `ENROLL_` / `REMOVE_ADMIN_CREDENTIAL`, content digest = `credential_change_digest` under that kind) is applied only through `propose_` / `approve_signed_admin_change` (family `ADMIN_CREDENTIAL`). `enroll_ca_admin_credential` refuses once `signed_admin_required` is set.
- Signed closing (0039): `status_change_challenge` purposes REJECT / WITHDRAW and `status_change_signature` role CLOSER. On a signing CA, `reject_` / `withdraw_certificate_status_change` refuse and `close_signed_status_change` closes the change.
- Signed CA administration (0037, ADR-0007): `ca_admin_credential` (admin WebAuthn keys, DBA-bootstrapped, append-only apart from the counter and removal; key and person disjoint from `officer_credential`), `ca_admin_challenge` (single-use, consumed once) and `ca_admin_signature` (append-only, MAKER/CHECKER per change and family). `ca_revocation_policy.signed_admin_required` (one-way) restricts the 0024/0028 changes to `propose_signed_admin_change` / `approve_signed_admin_change`.
- CA administration (0024): `ca_admin_grant` (CA admins, bootstrapped by the DBA) and append-only `ca_admin_change`. Registering the issuer certificate and granting or removing revocation officers need a proposal by one CA admin and approval of the exact content digest by another (`propose_`/`approve_`/`reject_ca_admin_change`); nobody grants themselves an officer role; `trust_ca_admin` has no direct `register_ca_issuer`.
- Lock order (0025): every status-change function locks the certificate row before the officer grant (`tests/revocation_lock_order.sh`); `certificate_status_change_supersession` indexes `(ca_id, serial_der_content, status, decided_at)` for the supersession probes of `crl_due` and `overdue_status_changes`.
- Schema-change audit (0026): event triggers (`ENABLE ALWAYS`) append every DDL command and object grant (command tag, object type and identity, session user, transaction id; no SQL text; temporary objects skipped) to `schema_change_audit`, owned by the NOLOGIN `trust_schema_audit_owner`. `schema_change_review` records which reviewed migration acknowledged changes up to which sequence number (`acknowledge_schema_changes`, superuser only); `unreviewed_schema_changes()` (granted to `trust_crl_operator`) counts the rest. Both tables are append-only, also in replica mode.
- Audit chain (0027): `audit_chain_entry` (seq, source, source key, `event_sha256`, `previous`, `entry_hash`), owned by the NOLOGIN `trust_audit_owner`, append-only. `seal_audit_chain` (`trust_audit_sealer`) links unsealed `audit_event` and `schema_change_audit` rows; `audit_chain_entries` and `unsealed_audit_records` (`trust_audit_verifier`) feed the external verifier (`docs/operations/lab-audit-chain.md`).
- Officer credentials (0028, ADR-0007 step 1): `officer_credential` binds a WebAuthn credential id and a P-256 key (compressed SEC1, canonicalised and curve-checked by the Rust core; its SHA-256 unique across all credentials ever enrolled) to an officer identity on a CA and a person identifier. One person holds one officer identity per CA and an identity's credentials belong to one person. Enrolment and removal need two distinct CA admins (`officer_credential_change`, approval of the exact length-prefixed content digest, no admin changing their own credentials). Only removal and a non-decreasing signature counter may change a row. Signed approvals follow in 0029.
- Signed approvals (0029, ADR-0007 step 2): `status_change_challenge` (single-use, at most 10 minutes, bound to the CA, registered issuer, change, exact content and acting officer; only the SHA-256 of a pgcrypto random value is kept) and append-only `status_change_signature` (claim digest, authenticator data, clientDataJSON, signature, credential, key digest, person, counter for maker and checker). `propose_`/`approve_signed_status_change` re-check everything but the ECDSA signature, which the Rust core verifies before calling them (`webauthn`). With `ca_revocation_policy.signed_approvals_required` (one-way) the unsigned propose/approve refuse; their bodies live on as `status_change_propose_unchecked` / `status_change_approve_unchecked`, granted to no service.
- `ca_issuer_certificate` (0022): the one issuer certificate (DER, SHA-256 generated) each CA signs CRLs with, registered once through `register_ca_issuer` after the Rust core checked it (`crl::check_crl_issuer`), immutable. `record_crl_intent` and `store_reconciled_crl` refuse (SQLSTATE 42704, `CrlError::IssuerMismatch`) unless given its fingerprint, so a CRL of another CA cannot be signed for or stored under a ca_id. Key rollover needs a new CA row (not implemented).
- `crl_publication`: issuer, `crl_number` (BIGINT, per CA, strictly increasing, below 2^63; formerly NUMERIC), thisUpdate, nextUpdate, plus (migration 0015) `status` (`RESERVED`, `INTENT_RECORDED`, `STORED`, `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, and from 0016 `ABANDONED`), `operation_ref`, `actor_id`, `entries_sha256`, `tbs_sha256`, `crl_der`, `expired_certs_on_crl_since`, and (0016) `intent_recorded_at`, set once by the intent. A `STORED` row is immutable. `abandon_stale_crl_reservations` retires `RESERVED` rows older than a threshold: they never recorded an intent, so they were never signed, and no intent can be recorded for them afterwards; their numbers stay taken. `reserve_crl` (0016) fails closed when the database clock is behind the CA's latest thisUpdate, so thisUpdate never decreases with the CRL number. `reserved_at` (0020) is the exact reservation time; publication coverage (`crl_change_covered`, `overdue_status_changes`) compares it with `decided_at` because thisUpdate is truncated to the second. `quarantine_stale_crl_intents` measures age from `intent_recorded_at` (from thisUpdate for intents recorded before 0016). `record_crl_intent` refuses an intent whose entries digest (computed by the Rust CA core from the reservation it signs), thisUpdate, nextUpdate or ExpiredCertsOnCRL date differ from the reservation, and (0017, replaced in 0020) one for any CRL number lower than the CA's highest reservation (intents and reservations are serialised per CA), for a reservation at least `crl_issue_interval` old (default 24 hours) or whose own nextUpdate has passed. From 0021 these "reserve again" refusals, and an abandoned reservation, use SQLSTATE 55000 (`CrlError::ReserveAgain`: nothing signed); 23514 (`ReplayedOrAmbiguousIntent`) is kept for replay and content mismatch. A `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` row that recorded an intent can be stored by `store_reconciled_crl` (0021) once the Rust core has verified the DER again against the reservation rebuilt from the snapshot and the TBS hash equals the one journaled before signing (`crl.reconciled` audit with the reconciling actor); rows quarantined before any intent stay final. "Stored" is not "published" by itself: from 0030, `published_crl` (granted only to `trust_crl_publisher`) returns the latest `STORED` CRL and the registered issuer certificate to the lab distribution point, which re-verifies it before serving (`docs/operations/lab-crl-distribution.md`).
- Expired entries (0031): `certificate.not_after` is recorded once at issuance (8-argument `store_signed_certificate`, value taken by the Rust core from the certificate it verified; immutable; NULL for older rows). With `ca_revocation_policy.keep_expired_on_crl` false (refused for ETSI EN 319 411-2), `reserve_crl` leaves out an entry once a stored CRL issued after its `not_after` has listed it (RFC 5280 3.3); `crl_change_covered` counts such pruning as coverage. From 0032, `crl_pruned_entries` (granted to `trust_crl_signer`) lists the revocations a reservation leaves out, and the Rust core refuses to record the intent unless each stored certificate DER confirms its serial and a notAfter equal to the stored `not_after` and before thisUpdate (`crl::check_pruned_entry`, `CrlError::UnexplainedOmission`).
- `crl_entry_snapshot`: the revoked entries frozen when a CRL number is reserved, keyed by (CA, CRL number, serial); immutable.
- `audit_event`: actor, request correlation, policy version, decision, timestamp and integrity metadata.

Lab migrations 0001-0043 exist; no production schema is implemented. Transactional consistency cannot encompass an external HSM signature. Persist intent and reconcile ambiguous outcomes.
