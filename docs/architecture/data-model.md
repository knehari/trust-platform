# Conceptual persistence model — v0.1

Entities:
- `ca`: immutable CA identity and active policy reference.
- `certificate_request`: requester, CSR fingerprint, idempotency key, status and policy decision.
- `serial_reservation`: unique (issuer_id, serial_bytes), allocated before signing, never reused.
- `certificate`: issuer, serial, DER fingerprint, lifecycle status and publication state.
- `revocation`: certificate identity, reason (0-6, 9, 10; never 7 or 8), invalidity/revocation timestamps (both limited to 1970-01-01 .. 9999-12-31, revocation date from migration 0016; the invalidity date is never after the proposal or decision; so every CRL can encode them), `change_id` of the approved status change. Definitive revocations (reason other than 6) are irreversible; a hold (6) may be released or upgraded. Status transitions go through `certificate_status_change` (maker-checker, migration 0014). From 0018 the lifecycle audit decision names the change and reason (`REVOKE:applied:change=<uuid>:reason=4`).
- Privileges (0023, ADR-0006 part A): the revocation/CRL tables and functions are owned by the NOLOGIN role `trust_revocation_owner`; service functions are `SECURITY DEFINER` with `search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp`; the capability roles `trust_revocation_officer_api`, `trust_crl_signer`, `trust_crl_operator` and `trust_ca_admin` hold EXECUTE on their functions only (plus SELECT on `ca_revocation_policy` / `crl_publication` for the officer API / signer) and no table DML. Superusers and the owner still bypass this.
- `ca_issuer_certificate` (0022): the one issuer certificate (DER, SHA-256 generated) each CA signs CRLs with, registered once through `register_ca_issuer` after the Rust core checked it (`crl::check_crl_issuer`), immutable. `record_crl_intent` and `store_reconciled_crl` refuse (SQLSTATE 42704, `CrlError::IssuerMismatch`) unless given its fingerprint, so a CRL of another CA cannot be signed for or stored under a ca_id. Key rollover needs a new CA row (not implemented).
- `crl_publication`: issuer, `crl_number` (BIGINT, per CA, strictly increasing, below 2^63; formerly NUMERIC), thisUpdate, nextUpdate, plus (migration 0015) `status` (`RESERVED`, `INTENT_RECORDED`, `STORED`, `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, and from 0016 `ABANDONED`), `operation_ref`, `actor_id`, `entries_sha256`, `tbs_sha256`, `crl_der`, `expired_certs_on_crl_since`, and (0016) `intent_recorded_at`, set once by the intent. A `STORED` row is immutable. `abandon_stale_crl_reservations` retires `RESERVED` rows older than a threshold: they never recorded an intent, so they were never signed, and no intent can be recorded for them afterwards; their numbers stay taken. `reserve_crl` (0016) fails closed when the database clock is behind the CA's latest thisUpdate, so thisUpdate never decreases with the CRL number. `reserved_at` (0020) is the exact reservation time; publication coverage (`crl_change_covered`, `overdue_status_changes`) compares it with `decided_at` because thisUpdate is truncated to the second. `quarantine_stale_crl_intents` measures age from `intent_recorded_at` (from thisUpdate for intents recorded before 0016). `record_crl_intent` refuses an intent whose entries digest (computed by the Rust CA core from the reservation it signs), thisUpdate, nextUpdate or ExpiredCertsOnCRL date differ from the reservation, and (0017, replaced in 0020) one for any CRL number lower than the CA's highest reservation (intents and reservations are serialised per CA), for a reservation at least `crl_issue_interval` old (default 24 hours) or whose own nextUpdate has passed. From 0021 these "reserve again" refusals, and an abandoned reservation, use SQLSTATE 55000 (`CrlError::ReserveAgain`: nothing signed); 23514 (`ReplayedOrAmbiguousIntent`) is kept for replay and content mismatch. A `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` row that recorded an intent can be stored by `store_reconciled_crl` (0021) once the Rust core has verified the DER again against the reservation rebuilt from the snapshot and the TBS hash equals the one journaled before signing (`crl.reconciled` audit with the reconciling actor); rows quarantined before any intent stay final. "Stored" is not "published": no distribution is implemented.
- `crl_entry_snapshot`: the revoked entries frozen when a CRL number is reserved, keyed by (CA, CRL number, serial); immutable.
- `audit_event`: actor, request correlation, policy version, decision, timestamp and integrity metadata.

Lab migrations 0001-0023 exist; no production schema is implemented. Transactional consistency cannot encompass an external HSM signature. Persist intent and reconcile ambiguous outcomes.
